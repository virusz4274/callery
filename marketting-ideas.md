# 🛡️ Built-In Security You Can Trust

At Callery, your privacy isn't an afterthought—it's built into our core architecture. We leverage **Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure** to ensure your voice recordings remain private, encrypted, and accessible only to you.

## 🔒 Professional-Grade Privacy

### **1. Bank-Level Encryption at Rest**
Every recording you save is automatically encrypted the moment it hits our storage. We use AES-256 encryption—the global standard for protecting sensitive data—to ensure your conversations are shielded from unauthorized access.

### **2. Private-By-Default Storage**
Unlike many cloud services, our storage architecture is locked down by default. Our "Closed Door" policy means your files are invisible to the public internet and search engines, accessible only through our secure, authenticated application layer.

### **3. Short-Lived "Magic" Access**
When you play back a recording, our system generates a unique, temporary digital key. This key:
- **Self-Expiring**: Valid only for your current session and expires within 60 minutes.
- **On-Demand Only**: Created only when you need it, ensuring your data stays behind a vault when not in use.
- **Ultra-Secure**: Built using modern cryptographic standards to maintain absolute privacy.
